# Public-Radio Apps and Streams

Listener-funded radio: funded by donations and grants, not by interrupting your song every four minutes to sell you a mattress. These are the stations people leave on for entire workdays. Every entry streams free in a browser or with a free app.

## NPR

**[npr.org](https://www.npr.org/)**

NPR's music side covers live concerts, genre specials, tiny-desk style sessions, and the free NPR One app for gentle radio curation. If "public radio" is a way of listening to you — talk segments, tasteful music picks, quietly smart reporting between songs — this is home base.

**The Fine Print:**
- Much of the music programming is curated segments rather than full-album jukebox play.
- The app asks for donations. That's how public radio works — it's the machine that keeps the mattresses out.

## KEXP

**[kexp.org](https://www.kexp.org/)** · **[Listen live](https://www.kexp.org/listen/)**

Seattle's beloved listener-powered powerhouse — DJs with real taste, thousands of live in-studio sessions archived for free on YouTube, and a free app that's better than many paid ones. Weekend 1990s blocks, local Seattle deep-dives, global electronic sessions — whatever tickles you, real humans picked it.

**The Fine Print:**
- Funding drives are, um, frequent. Fair enough — they're listener-funded by design.
- In-studio session videos live on YouTube, so expect standard YouTube ads there.

## YourClassical (MPR / APM)

**[yourclassical.org](https://www.yourclassical.org/)** · **[Listen](https://www.yourclassical.org/listen)**

Classical Minnesota Public Radio's streaming arm, and the calm-audio gem of this directory. Multiple free 24/7 streams: the main classical channel, opera, **"Peaceful Classical"**, and piano-only loops that work perfectly as a low-effort focus soundtrack.

**The Fine Print:**
- Streams are continuous mixes — pieces flow into each other with occasional host talk at the edges. There is no on-demand song picker.
- The mobile experience is smoother inside the main MPR/YourClassical app than in a bare browser tab.

## WFMT

**[wfmt.com](https://www.wfmt.com/)**

Chicago's classical flagship: long-form classical programming, opera broadcasts, and real announcers who tell you what you just heard — radio done the respectful old way. Free web stream, no ads inside the music.

**The Fine Print:**
- Long-form means long: an evening opera broadcast is a commitment, not background.
- Rather than ads, you'll hear listener-support mentions. That's the public-radio way, and honestly, it's fair.

## Radio Garden

**[radio.garden](https://radio.garden/)**

A spinning globe covered in live radio stations, tuned by dragging the earth around. Land somewhere and instantly hear local radio from that exact place — world music, news voices, stations you would never encounter on any app chart. It's the prettiest free music toy on the internet, and it costs nothing and requires no account.

**The Fine Print:**
- Station availability wobbles: individual stations go quiet for a few days while being fixed, then reappear.
- It streams over the internet all the time — fine on Wi-Fi, mind your data plan on mobile.
