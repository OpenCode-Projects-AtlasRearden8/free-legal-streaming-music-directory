# Start Here: Free & Legal Music Streaming, Sorted

This directory collects **free and legal** ways to hear music and calm audio — no subscriptions required, ever. Each entry gets a warm blurb and an honest "The Fine Print" section, because free tiers are great but they're never *just* free, and you deserve to know exactly where the catches are.

> Looking for audiobooks or pure sleep sounds? That's our neighbor directories, not us. We love them, but this page is strictly **music**.

## The five categories

1. **[Library streaming passes](library-streaming.md)** — Hoopla, Freegal, and Naxos.
   Your library card is secretly one of the best music keys in the world. Full albums to stream (and often download to keep), zero ads, zero subscription, just an annual or monthly play limit your doorstep librarian will explain.

2. **[Public-radio apps and streams](public-radio.md)** — NPR, KEXP, YourClassical, WFMT, Radio Garden.
   Listener-funded radio whose entire sales pitch is that they don't have a sales pitch. Great DJs, no commercial ads, and composer-curated classical streams you can run all day while you work.

3. **[Live coding-game stations](coding-gaming-stations.md)** — Rainwave, SomaFM, Chillhop, LoFi Café, Lofi Girl, OC ReMix.
   Station-flavored audio built for focus: video-game-score voting stations, endless lofi and chillhop radio, YouTube's famous live lofi loop. Pick one, mute your tabs, and get things done.

4. **[Archives for older music](older-music-archives.md)** — Internet Archive, 78 RPM collection, National Jukebox, IMSLP.
   Music that has outlived its copyright (or is gracefully public domain). Millions of historical recordings from the 1890s onward, streamed or downloaded straight from libraries and archives.

5. **[Royalty-free players](royalty-free-music.md)** — Free Music Archive, ccMixter, Pixabay, Incompetech, Bensound, Musopen.
   Music you're explicitly *licensed* to use in your own videos, podcasts, or games — for free. This is the category where you must read the fine print, so we've done it for you.

## Five-minute picker

- "I have 3 minutes." Open [Radio Garden](public-radio.md#radio-garden) and spin the globe.
- "I want a full album tonight." Open [Hoopla via your library](library-streaming.md#hoopla).
- "I need to focus for 6 hours." Open [SomaFM's Groove Salad](coding-gaming-stations.md#somafm) and only resurface when the work's done.
- "I need background music for my YouTube video." Open [Free Music Archive](royalty-free-music.md#free-music-archive-fma).
- "Show me something weird from 1920." Open [the Internet Archive's 78 RPM collection](older-music-archives.md#the-78-rpm-records-collection).

## The honest overall gotchas

- **"Free" still has a business model.** Ads (YouTube live radio), listener donations (KEXP, SomaFM), or limits on plays (Freegal's 5 downloads a week). We itemize all three in each category.
- **Catalogs rotate.** Especially on library and royalty-free services, music comes and goes. If an album vanished, it isn't you.
- **"No account" vs. "no library card" differ.** Some services are anonymous (SomaFM, Radio Garden); some unlock their full value only after you sign up with a library card. The blurbs are sorted so you know which is which before you click.
- **Commercial use is its own question.** "Free to listen" is not "free to put in an ad." Only the [royalty-free](royalty-free-music.md) entries cover that, and each has its own license terms.
