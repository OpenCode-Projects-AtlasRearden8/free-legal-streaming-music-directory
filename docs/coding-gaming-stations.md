# Live Coding-Game Stations

Stations built around what you're *doing* while you listen — coding, gaming, deep focus. Video-game scores with community voting, noncommercial chiptune and chill radio, endless lofi. Everything here plays free, no subscription.

## SomaFM

**[somafm.com](https://somafm.com/)** · **[Groove Salad](https://somafm.com/groovesalad/)**

A listener-supported, noncommercial, independent internet radio station from San Francisco that's been quietly running since 2000. 30+ handcrafted channels: *Groove Salad* (the legendary ambient/downtempo work companion), *Deep Space One*, *Secret Agent*, *Lush*, *Digitalis*... The channel descriptions alone are worth reading. No accounts, no login, just pick a channel and press play.

*Why it's here:* it's the single best "put this on for eight hours and forget it exists" directory entry in this whole folder. Groove Salad is the calm-audio channel of record.

**The Fine Print:**
- Listener-supported: the twice-yearly pledge drives come around, and they're honest that listener money keeps it alive. That's really the whole catch — SomaFM sells nothing.
- Mobile apps are third-party (look for SomaFM in your radio app), though desktop streaming is dead-simple in any browser.

## Rainwave

**[rainwave.cc](https://rainwave.cc/)**

Community interactive radio for video-game music, anime soundtracks, and remixes — with a live, ongoing listening queue: songs spin up by community vote, you can request tracks and spend votes to push yours forward, and watch the chat debate the canon in real time. It's a station you *participate in*, live, while coding a side project or gaming alongside it.

**The Fine Print:**
- Democracy means you won't control your own playlist. Learn to trust the crowd, or wait out a losing vote.
- Requests and vote features work best once you've made a (free) account — but plain listening is open from the very first click.

## Chillhop Music

**[chillhop.com](https://chillhop.com/)**

The famous chillhop/lofi-hip-hop radio built on a real, licensed catalog of their own — not a YouTube-shuffle rerun. Their free radio lives on the website and their YouTube channel, with browsable playlists by mood: "jazzy & mellow," "chill study beats," "sleepy." Made by a small independent team running since 2013, and the catalog quality shows.

**The Fine Print:**
- The YouTube version carries standard YouTube ads. Their own web player doesn't.
- It is *continuously* streaming — no tracklist control, no pre-save library; if the current track isn't for you, the next swing comes around soon.

## LoFi Café

**[lofi.cafe](https://lofi.cafe/)**

A quiet, gorgeous minimalist website that is nothing but an endless lofi radio stream — a 'café' vibe, calm visual, a volume button. This is the zero-click, zero-login calm stream for when you just opened your laptop to code and don't want to pick anything.

**The Fine Print:**
- One link, one stream, no settings: it plays anonymously and forever, supported by listener donations. That's really the entire catch.
- One channel only. If lofi is not the flavor of calm you wanted, it's a hard no.

## Lofi Girl (YouTube live radio)

**[youtube.com/@LofiGirl](https://www.youtube.com/@LofiGirl)**

The famous "lofi hip hop radio – beats to relax/study to" livestream loop, live around the clock since the 2017 original. There's nothing *on* it: a girl studying with her cat, and beats. And hundreds of millions of study hours can't be wrong.

**The Fine Print:**
- It's YouTube, so ads run periodically. That's the honest price of the most famous free study stream in the world.
- Works best as background; you can't skip or queue.

## OverClocked ReMix (OC ReMix)

**[ocremix.org](https://www.ocremix.org/)**

Less a *station*, more a giant free archive of thousands of fan-made remixes of video-game music, peer-reviewed for quality before each ReMix is posted. Browse by game, by composer, or by era — this is ~25 years of a fan project that turned into free art. It even has built-in streaming playlists, so it functions like a station you steer yourself.

**The Fine Print:**
- The original game music is owned by the game studios (Nintendo et al.), so OC ReMix is an exercise in transformative art and goodwill. Bottom line: free to listen, stream, and download — **not** licensed for commercial use.
- No "radio DJ" between songs; it's you browsing.
